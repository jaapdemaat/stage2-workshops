Overview

Workshops: 25  ·  Sessions: 39  ·  Length: 3 hours each  ·  Places: limited, numbers to be confirmed

These optional workshops sit alongside your units rather than replacing them. Block 1 workshops help you design and publish your Unit 5 critical report and document your work for Open Studio. Block 2 workshops support your Unit 7 Practices and your portfolio. Platform workshops are organised separately by your Platform leaders.

Before you book
  • Optional and additional. These workshops are extra to your units. You choose which ones, if any, to attend.
  • First come, first served. Places are limited (numbers to be confirmed for each workshop) and go in the order people book.
  • Sign up through Microsoft Bookings only. Use the Book button on each workshop. Emails, Teams messages or turning up without a booking don't secure a place.
  • Booking is a commitment. Only book sessions you're sure you can attend. Not turning up without cancelling isn't an option.
  • Can't attend? Cancel at least 24 hours before. Use the cancel link in the confirmation email you receive when you book. That way the place can be offered to someone else.

Photograph flat work and models so they look as good on screen as they do on the table.

Good documentation makes the difference between strong work and strong work that nobody can see. This session covers how to photograph flat work and 3D pieces clearly and consistently, and how to process a set of images in Lightroom so they're ready for Open Studio, reports and portfolios.

What you'll cover

  • Camera settings and lighting for photographing flat work
  • Photographing 3D objects and models
  • Importing into Lightroom Classic
  • White balance, cropping and syncing settings across a set
  • Export settings for screen, PDF and print

FAQ

Do I have to go to these workshops?

No. They're optional and additional to your units. Pick the ones that help with what you're making.

The session is full. What now?

Check whether there's a repeat later in the programme, watch the suggested LinkedIn Learning course, or book a 1-to-1 with the technician.

Intro or Intermediate?

Intro assumes you've never used the software. Intermediate assumes you know the basics, for example from the Intro session or a LinkedIn Learning course.

What should I bring?

Your laptop and charger, the Adobe apps installed through UAL, and the files you're working on. Working on your own project makes the session much more useful.

I booked but can't make it.

Cancel through your Microsoft Bookings confirmation email at least 24 hours before the session, so the place can be offered to someone else. Booking is a commitment: not turning up without cancelling isn't an option.

Can I just turn up?

Only if you've booked. Sign-up is through Microsoft Bookings only, and places go first come, first served.
